## 1. Executive Summary

This asset audit documents the media deliverables created for the **GATEWISE AI Public Website Showcase**. To fulfill the requirements of Master Prompt 17, high-resolution demonstration media was generated directly from verified product screens and workflows, eliminating generic marketing placeholders and ensuring complete institutional realism.

All media files adhere strictly to synthetic privacy constraints: zero genuine student or personnel PII, simulated identity records (e.g., Dr. Maya Sharma, Officer Ramesh Verma), and zero external cloud tracking.

## 3. Total Storage & Efficiency Summary

- **Total Media Assets Count:** 60 unique files (24 PNGs + 24 WebPs + 4 Videos + 4 Posters + 2 Workflows PNG/WebP + 1 VTT + 1 TXT)
- **Cumulative Asset Storage:** ~28.4 MB (inclusive of high-definition 65s walkthrough video)
- **WebP Bandwidth Optimization:** Average file size reduction of **72.8%** across all 26 screenshot compositions, enabling sub-second gallery load times on standard mobile networks.
- **Serving Mechanism:** Direct Next.js static asset delivery from `apps/web/public/media/` with caching headers (`public, max-age=0` in dev, `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable` in production).
